OnePiecedle: frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about LogPose, the daily One Piece game.

What is OnePiecedle?

OnePiecedle is the daily One Piece game: seven mystery characters to guess every day, across seven different modes (Classic, Wanted, Silhouette, Devil Fruit, Emoji, Opening, Volume). Free, no sign-up, straight in your browser. The site is called LogPose.

How do you play OnePiecedle?

Pick a mode, guess a One Piece character, and the clues open up with every try: affiliation, origin, bounty, Devil Fruit, Haki. The colors and arrows show how close the day's answer is. The game starts right away, no sign-up.

Is the game free?

Yes — LogPose is 100% free, with no sign-up and no download. It plays right in your browser, on mobile and desktop alike.

How many characters and modes are there?

The game features 258 One Piece characters and seven daily modes: Classic, Wanted, Silhouette, Devil Fruit, Emoji, Opening and Volume. There is also an Infinite mode, for unlimited practice games.

Is there a new character to guess every day?

Yes. Each mode has a new mystery character daily, the same for every player. Past days stay playable from the answers calendar, and the streak carries on by coming back each day.

Can you play OnePiecedle with friends?

Yes — the 1v1 Versus mode lets you challenge a friend in a private room on the same mystery character, in Best-of-1, Best-of-3 or Best-of-5.
